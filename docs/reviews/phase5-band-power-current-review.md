4. **Plan fit and scope.** The calculation follows [§6.6/SPE-002](../../plan.md#L1161) by providing a caller-selected whole-bin integrated-power primitive. It follows [§7.10](../../plan.md#L1688) by converting dBm values to linear power before summation, and [§12.7](../../plan.md#L3222) by preserving below-detection, clipped, and unobserved states as unknown rather than numeric zero. It does not map Wi-Fi channel identifiers to bands or provide the other SPE-002 views, so the relevant obligation correctly remains `IN_PROGRESS`.

5. **Numerical conversion and rounding.** In [band_power.rs](../../crates/spectrum-contract/src/band_power.rs#L252), integer milli-dBm is converted with `10^(milli_dBm / 10000)` to mW (or mW/Hz). dBm/bin contributions are summed directly in mW; dBm/Hz contributions are multiplied by the validated uniform grid-bin width in Hz before summation. The total is converted once with `10000 * log10(sum_mW)`, then rounded to an integer milli-dBm; `round()` implements half-way-away-from-zero for representable ties. The checked fixtures confirm two −30 dBm bins produce −26.990 dBm and 0 dBm/Hz over one 1 MHz bin produces +60 dBm.

6. **Range, coverage, and unknown handling.** The function rejects empty, out-of-grid, or misaligned half-open ranges, accepts both outer grid edges, and selects `[start_index, stop_index)` only. It returns no total if any selected bin is below detection, clipped, or not observed; unknown results carry source-grid-ordered causes. Observed coverage includes clipped bins, while exact coverage counts only observed and unclipped bins.

7. **Overflow and resource bounds.** Offsets, indices, counts, and conversions use checked operations, and selected work must fit both `max_bins_per_sweep` and `max_work_units`. The validated model bounds frequency to `1e12` Hz, power fields to −500 through +200 dBm units (dBm/bin or dBm/Hz according to `PowerUnit`), and bin count to `u32`. Even the loose upper bound formed by multiplying the maximum dBm/Hz value by the maximum bin width and maximum `u32` bin count remains far below `f64` overflow; the minimum contribution also remains above underflow. The default limits are much lower (16,384 bins and 2,000,000 work units). The output blocker vector is bounded by selected work.

8. **API, schema, and identity compatibility.** The change adds a module and public Rust types only. Sweep/event models, serde schemas, event rules, and dependencies are unchanged. The derived result is not serialized as canonical evidence and does not mutate the input sweep; a regression checks the sweep SHA-256 before and after calculation.
